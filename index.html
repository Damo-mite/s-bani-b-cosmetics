What shea butter is

A thick moisturiser

Shea butter is mostly fat from the shea nut. On skin and hair it sits as a rich layer and helps slow water loss.

Naturally rich

Unrefined shea contains vitamins A and E, along with a small share of unsaponifiable compounds that refined butter loses.

From West Africa

The shea tree, Vitellaria paradoxa, grows across the savanna belt. These tubs are labeled as sourced from Ghana.

Whipped to spread

Whipping folds air into the butter, so it feels lighter under the fingers than a solid block.
